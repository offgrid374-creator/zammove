# ZamMove: handoff notes for Claude

Read this first. It records what the system is, how to work on it safely, what was built recently, and what is still open. Last updated 2026-11-26, after the audit fixes and error-recovery features (branch `integrate-redesign`).

## 1. What ZamMove is

A web system for Zamboanga City with two businesses:

- **Hauling:** a customer books a van or truck with a driver. Drivers are assigned vehicles owned by vehicle owners. ZamMove takes a service fee and commission.
- **Self-drive rental:** a customer rents a vehicle (no driver) directly from its owner, through ZamMove.

Roles: customer, driver, vehicle owner (car owner), cashier, admin (super/admin/sub). One account can hold several roles and switches workspace ("mode").

It is a school Software Engineering project (SE Group 1). The user is the developer/student. Progress reports live in `docs/` (Progress Report No. 2 PDF is the latest submitted copy).

### Frontend architecture (important)

- Plain JavaScript files loaded in order (`js/01` … `js/25`). There is no bundler.
- **Each layer wraps the global `render()`** (`const _r = render; render = function () { … }`) and often redraws parts of the page. A later file can override an earlier screen.
- Each layer has its own `document.addEventListener('click', …, true)` capture handler and reacts to `data-a="<action>"` attributes. Pick a unique prefix per file (`zb-` trips, `z7-` 17-improve, `z23-` cases, `zr-` report button, `fx-` rentals, `vz-` verification, `w-` workspaces, `zo-` ops).
- Data comes from `S` (the state from `/api/state`); call the server with `ZAPI.act(name, payload)` then `ZAPI.refresh()` / `render()`.
- Globals worth knowing: `S`, `view` (customer page), `S.ws` / `S.wp[ws]` (workspace + page), `msg`, `esc`, `peso`, `toast`, `img`, `ZPAY` (payment math).
- Several "cards" are injected into a `.z7-box` by `17-improve.js` with extra content from `window.ZM_CASES` (`23-cases.js`).

## 5. Business rules (current behaviour)

### Booking (hauling)
Statuses: `Pending → Accepted → On the Way to Pickup → Awaiting Down Payment → Down Payment Confirmed → Items Loaded → In Transit → Arrived at Destination → Delivery Confirmation → Awaiting Final Payment → Completed` (+ `Cancelled`, `Rejected`, `No driver found`, `Interrupted`).
- Unaccepted bookings time out (`cfg.pendMin`, default 30 min) to "No driver found" (in `h.sweep`).
- Cancel: free until the driver heads to pickup; afterwards 10% fee to the driver. Free if the driver is >15 min late (+ strike).
- **No-show:** after the driver waits 15 min at pickup (`NOSHOW_MIN`), `noShow` closes the booking with a 10% fee to the driver. Customer can `noShowDispute`; a disputed fee does not block new bookings; staff decide with `moneyDecide`.
- **Reschedule:** `reschedule` is free before the driver sets off (max 3). The driver keeps the job only if free at the new time, else it reopens.
- **Payments (down 50% at pickup, balance after delivery):** the customer first declares how they pay with `payDeclare` (GCash part + reference, cash part, cash handed over → change). The driver's `driverPay` must match the declaration, or the driver records it with `force` + a note (customer and staff are told). Every payment record carries a `tx` object (due, g, c, rcv, chg, ref, decl, match, diff, why) that the receipt shows. Without a declaration the driver records alone (old behaviour).
- Pre-departure verification (`verification.js`): Start PIN/QR from the customer, plate check (first typo only warns; a second mismatch reports a discrepancy; the right plate auto-resolves), cargo checklist (a different count first asks for a recount), both confirm, then `vAuthorize` → In Transit. Delivery needs the customer's Delivery PIN (`dDeliver`).
- Wrong change: customer can report within 24 h of a cash payment (see reports below).

### Rental
Statuses: `Pending → Approved → Active → Returned → Completed` (+ `Cancelled`, `Rejected`).
- Customer can pay only after approval. Payments go **to the owner**: owner adds a GCash number (`ownerGcash`); renter pays by GCash to it or cash at pickup; **owner confirms** each payment (`payVerify`, sets `coll`). Old GCash payments to ZamMove are verified by the cashier.
- Contract issued on approval (frozen terms + SHA-256; `CONTRACT_V = 5`); both sign (screen or paper photo) before release.
- Release: owner records odometer, fuel, 6 photos.
- **Return:** renter presses "Return the vehicle" → `rentalReturn` with 6 photos, odometer, fuel, phone location (distance to return point) and photo file ages (flags, not proof). Owner checks within 24 h (`RET_CHECK_H`): "Everything matches" (`match:true`), "Something is different" (owner readings + note → renter confirms/disputes), or "Ask for new photos" (`retake`). No answer in 24 h → accepted automatically. Owner can still record a return themselves.
- Claim window 3 days (`CLAIM_DAYS`); with no open claim the rental **auto-completes** after it.
- Deposit refund: the owner refunds directly (`rentalRefund`), renter confirms (`rentalRefundAck`) or disputes. Reminder at 24 h, staff alert at 48 h.
- **Cancellation:** free ≥48 h before pickup (8:00 AM on start date); within 48 h the owner keeps one day's fee; refunds created automatically (`kind: 'refund'`, `from: owner`).
- **Expiry:** Pending unanswered 24 h → cancelled; Approved not fully paid 12 h before pickup → cancelled, full refund.
- **Date change / extension:** `rentalChange` (Pending applies at once; Approved same-or-longer; Active extend only) → owner `rentalChangeAnswer`; accepted changes are added to the contract as amendments (`r.ct.amend`).
- Owner money: `h.ownerBal` nets owner trip earnings, rental share, rental cash the owner holds, refunds sent, payouts, and remittances. Owners remit ZamMove's commission weekly (`ownerRemitSend`, cashier verifies). Owing for 14 days pauses their rental listings (`u.rentPaused`).

### Reports and disputes
- Reports (`createIncident`, wrong change `changeDispute`) are **drafts** (`st: 'Unconfirmed'`) visible only to the reporter until `caseConfirm`. `caseWithdraw` dismisses a draft or withdraws a sent report until ZamMove decides (lifts driver flags). Drafts are discarded after 24 h. Emergencies (`emergency`) still alert everyone immediately.
- The floating "Report a problem" button (`25-report.js`) is the only report entry during a trip; on phones it is a round "!" icon.
- Cases list lives on the customer **Report page**, not Home. The wrong-change form is a closed section on the Report page.
- Money disputes (renter says "I did pay", refund never arrived, no-show fee) are decided by the cashier on **Payments to verify → Money disputes** (`moneyDecide`).
- Home page is intentionally clean: no cases/attention cards (they moved to Report, Bookings and Track).

Built from `nov 25 consultation notes.md` (a list from the user's adviser). New files: backend `domain/catalog.js`, `domain/people.js`; frontend `js/26-catalog.js`, `27-people.js`, `28-extras.js`, `29-money.js`, `shared/reports.js`, admin `admin-cat.js`, `admin-rep.js`.
- **Admin catalog** (Admin panel → System → Catalog & fuel; stored in `S.cfg.cat`): brands → models (usual type, seats, fuel), fuel types incl. **Hybrid**, colours, transmissions. Add / rename / hide / delete (in-use entries can only be hidden). Vehicle registration uses linked dropdowns (brand → only its models) with "Other (not listed)" → `catReq`, shown to the admin to add.

- **Filters / preferences**: rental page filters in order: Who drives? - **Owner-managed people**: drivers apply to an owner (`apply` with `employer`) → status `Waiting for Owner` → `ownerAppDecide` → normal admin pipeline; approved drivers get `u.employer` and work for that owner only (`h.driverOwner`). Owners see only their drivers. Owners register helpers (`ownerHelper`, stored on the owner user); a booking with helpers needs `bookHelpers` before departure (added to the verification conditions); customers see helper names.
- **Reservations** (`createBooking` with `ret`): outbound booking carries both fares + daily hold fee (`b.pkg`, `S.cfg.holdFee`, default ₱500); a linked prepaid pick-up booking (`pkgOf`, tot 0) is auto-assigned to the same driver on accept; the vehicle is held between (`h.heldOn`); zero-balance steps auto-confirm. Cancelling the outbound cancels both.
- **Booking groups** (`grp`): "Add a vehicle or drop-off" on a booking card pre-fills a new booking linked to the same move.
- **Signup barangay** (`brgy`, `loc`); booking form shows vehicles within 5 km.
- **Delivery QR**: customer's delivery panel shows a QR (`ZMD:<booking>:<PIN>`); driver can scan (BarcodeDetector) or type the PIN; Start/Delivery PINs also sent by simulated SMS + email.

- **Owner discount** (`ownerDiscount`): up to 50% of the fare and the owner's share; ZamMove fee/commission stay on the full fare (`h.splitOf` subtracts it from `own`); lowers the final balance (`h.due`).
- **Ledger and reports**: booking cards show a payment history with running balance; `shared/reports.js` (`ZREP.view`) gives sales, daily cash vs GCash, receivables, discounts, trips, rentals, remittances, refunds — date range, search, totals, Print, CSV — in the admin panel (Money → Reports), owner Earnings (with today / date-range tiles) and cashier Income.

### Driver → owner → ZamMove (turnover)
Bookings created after this change carry `flow: 'owner'` (`domain/turnover.js`, `js/30-turnover.js`). Older bookings keep the old driver → ZamMove remittance.
- The customer pays the driver; GCash goes to the vehicle owner's number when set (`payTo` in the customer view, payments get `gto: 'owner'|'driver'`).
- The driver keeps their share and turns the rest over (`turnover`); the owner confirms (`turnoverDecide`); a "not received" can be contested (`turnoverDispute` → cashier `moneyDecide`). When the owner holds the driver's share (GCash), the owner pays the driver (`ownerPayDriver`). `h.turnBal(em)` > 0 = driver owes owner.
- The owner owes ZamMove's portion (`split.zam`: 5% fee + commission) of every completed new-flow trip (`h.ownerTripsOwed`), included in `ownerBal.due` and the weekly `ownerRemitSend`.
- Fees/charges follow their booking (`h.viaOwner`): on new-flow bookings, cancellation-fee credits and wrong-change/incident charges pass through the owner (`h.ownerCross`).
- A driver still owing their owner 7 days after a trip is overdue (blocked from new jobs); unturned money counts toward the ₱5,000 cash limit.
- Old-flow tests use `legacySetup` from `test/_setup.js`.
- Balances are **per owner** (a driver can drive vehicles of two owners): `h.turnBy(em)` → `{ownerEmail: bal}`; `turnover` takes `owner` (pkey). The owner checks GCash sent to their number (`ownerGcashCheck`); "not received" opens a dispute the cashier decides (`moneyDecide`, down/final kinds → customer pays again).

### Rental pickup/return check (`domain/handover.js`, `js/32-handover.js`, `test/handover.test.js`)
Rentals created from now on carry `hv: 1` (tests: `t.hv = true`; older tests drop it in `_setup.js`).
- Renter picks **pickup and return times** (`pt`, `rtm`, 6 AM–8 PM every 30 min; calendar days). They go into the contract (`CONTRACT_V = 6`), the cancellation/expiry clock (`h.rnPickupMs`) and the late charge (2 h grace after `rtm`).
- **Pickup** (opens 12 h before `pt`, once paid + signed): both share location (`hoLoc`; >300 m or missing → owner gives a `far` reason); renter shows a Release code (`hoIssue`: PIN + QR `ZMR1.<id>.<stage>.<pin>`, 30 min, also simulated SMS/email); owner types/scans it (`hoVerify`, 5 tries → 5 min lock); renter types the plate (`hoPlate`); owner records odometer/fuel/photos (`release` → `h.hoRelease`, stays Approved); renter confirms (`hoConfirm`, optional difference note) → Active.
- **With a driver**: the named driver (sees the rental in their view, Active trip page card) scans the code; renter confirms plate; driver presses Start (no readings).
- **Return**: renter shows a Return code; owner/driver scans it → renter's proof of return. The owner's `return` needs it, or a `nocode` reason (≥10 chars, renter told). Codes are stripped from every view (`noCodes` in views.js).

### Booking loophole fixes (`test/loophole.test.js`)
- Late driver (in `h.sweep`): at `LATE_MIN` (30) past pickup time the customer, driver, staff and owner are told once (`b.lateWarn`); still `Accepted` at `GHOST_MIN` (90) → strike, job taken back (`No driver found`, driver added to `declined`, linked reserved pick-up released, `b.ghosted`). A driver already "On the Way" is never replaced automatically.

## 9. Gotchas learned the hard way

- The app paints a colour stripe on every `<header>` and centers children of `[role=dialog]`: new pop-ups use a plain `div` header and `align-items: stretch`.
- Global `li.ok` and button margin styles leak into new components; use your own class names.
- A redraw from another layer can wipe your injected HTML; inject after `setTimeout(0)` or in the shared `.z7-box`.
- `h.ev(b, by, to, note)` changes the booking status; push to `b.ev` directly for a note without a status change.
- `paySum` counts only `Recorded by driver` and `Verified`; refunds use statuses `Due / Sent / Paid / Disputed`.
- Python-in-bash heredocs with typographic quotes have broken before; write longer patch scripts to a file first.
